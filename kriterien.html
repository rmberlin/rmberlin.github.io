What we stand for

Revolution Music Berlin collects concerts and parties in Berlin – punk, jazz, hip-hop, metal, techno, every genre. What connects us is one conviction: music is for everyone. We stand for freedom, diversity and respect: everyone should be able to enjoy music, no matter where they come from, what they believe, whom they love or what they look like.

Behind the site are a few people doing this in their free time – as volunteers, not to make money. There are no ads, no paid listings and no tracking; nobody earns anything here. We just want nice people to find good gigs, and Berlin’s scene to get something out of it.

How the list comes together

  • Places with a stance: clubs, bars and housing projects that look out themselves for who plays there. Their events show up automatically.
  • Looked at with care: for other places, a concert shows up once we have looked at at least one of the bands – their lyrics, interviews, labels and who they share the stage with.

The genre tags are found automatically and are only a hint.

Where we draw the line

We do not list events with artists who spread hatred against people or deny others their dignity – not even as a support act.

We never make such decisions alone, but always two of us and based on sources. We do not publish lists and do not judge anyone in public.

If a concert is missing, that says nothing about the band or the place. We simply do not see everything that happens in Berlin.

Join in

Is a concert missing, is something wrong, or do you know a place that fits here? Write to us via the contact link below – we are glad about every hint and read everything.

Where the data comes from

Straight from the venues’ websites and from the calendar radar.squat.net, where Stressfaktor publishes its dates too. All details without guarantee – check details and tickets with the venue. Venue locations for “Near me” come from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL). Some genre badges are based on tags from MusicBrainz (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0).
